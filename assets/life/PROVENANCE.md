# Environmental artwork

Created for this repository with the built-in image generator, September 26, 2026.

The sprite sheet requested isolated, transparent painterly winter figures, animals and physical objects, matching the existing warm cabin / blue winter palette. Twelve authored cells were cut at their measured bounds, trimmed and reduced to at most 300 pixels; alpha was retained and files encoded as WebP.

The empty-sofa plate used `assets/living-room.png` as an edit target: remove only the sleeping cats, reconstruct the wool blanket, preserve camera, composition, furniture and lighting. Only a feathered section over the sofa is used in the running application. Original scene assets remain unchanged.

The small render layer reuses the scene coordinate system, uses reduced lighting in dark rooms, and makes no network calls after asset loading.
